Deposit / Redeem

Standard ERC-4626 flows: approve the vault to spend the underlying, then deposit for shares (ws-SPY) — or redeem/withdraw shares back to underlying. Redeem and withdraw have no pause path in the protocol design: our own controls can never trap your funds.

Backing Invariant

coverage reads live from backingCoverage() on the vault at 0x3a1c83ABc79A512aAd68ac721CE0F10F41de3a01 (js/config.js); verify it yourself with any RPC client.

coverage = the vault's raw asset balance divided by the accounted figure (deposits plus credited yield — harvest credits raise it without minting shares), readable on-chain via backingCoverage(); 1e18 = exact cover (an empty vault reads 1e18: no accounted liability); above 1e18 is unaccounted excess (donations/uncredited yield); below 1.0 only after an issuer burn; redemptions are served until the balance is exhausted.
