Yield vaults for tokenized stocks.
Checkable, not sellable.

Wellstreet wraps on-chain stock tokens (starting with the S&P 500 ETF token "SPY" on Robinhood Chain, chain 4663) in open-source ERC-4626 vaults and routes liquidity-pool fee income to depositors. MIT-licensed and unaudited — the docs are written to be checkable against the code, not to sell anything.

Who holds the keys You. Vault shares are yours; redeems have no pause path.
Fee split 90% depositors / 10% protocol (timelock-settable, hard-capped at 20%).
Where the numbers come from Your browser, reading public RPC nodes. No backend, no API keys.
Audit status None. Read the invariants and the threat model instead.

Deposit / Redeem

Standard ERC-4626 flows: approve the vault to spend the underlying, then deposit for shares (ws-SPY) — or redeem/withdraw shares back to underlying. Redeem and withdraw have no pause path in the protocol design: our own controls can never trap your funds.
