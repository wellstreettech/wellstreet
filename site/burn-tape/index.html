    How a burn happens

    Vault-lane revenue splits by named constants — DEPOSITOR_BPS = 9000 and BURN_BPS = 1000 in src/RoamVault.sol: nine parts in ten raise depositors' share price, one part in ten is the burn cut. The dev take on this path is structurally zero — nothing is retained anywhere.

    The burn cut accumulates as accounted accrual on the roamer — conserved, never treasury, never junk-forwarded. sweepToBurn() is permissionless and nonReentrant (src/RoamingHarvester.sol): it swaps the accrual to WELL through the pinned router and transfers the WELL to the dEaD address, emitting one Burned event per token. A token that cannot route is skipped and the rest of the tail stays live — SweepSkipped carries the reason. No operator signature is needed for a sweep: anyone can fire it, and the tape exists whether or not we pull it.
